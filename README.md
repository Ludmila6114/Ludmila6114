## 🔬 My PhD project

My research focuses on **LTR (long terminal repeat) retrotransposons**, repetitive genetic elements that constitute a substantial fraction of eukaryotic genomes.

I am particularly interested in **Errantiviruses**, a clade of LTR retrotransposons in *Drosophila* with unusual virus-like properties. Unlike many transposable elements that are expressed directly in germline cells, Errantiviruses are expressed in somatic follicle cells surrounding the developing germline and can subsequently transfer into oocytes.

A particularly intriguing feature of Errantiviruses is an additional open reading frame encoding an **envelope-like (Env) protein** related to envelope proteins of F-type baculoviruses. However, the role of this protein and the cellular mechanisms enabling soma-to-germline transmission remain incompletely understood.

My PhD aims to address questions such as:

* Is the **Env protein** required for Errantivirus infectivity in the *Drosophila* ovary?
* Which cellular pathways are exploited during **soma-to-germline transfer**?
* What molecular mechanisms underlie Errantivirus infectivity?

Through this work, I aim to contribute to our understanding of **transposable element biology, host–parasite interactions, and virology**.
